THE PROJECT

From flavour discovery
to staff follow-up.

Eleeshas brings the menu, the customer’s scoop choices and the staff inbox together. In the local web prototype, staff review enquiries, confirm availability and reserve tracked stock. The Android-first source supports on-device drafts and a configurable API connection.

iOS and iPad are design targets. Hosted infrastructure, staff roles, backups, notifications and payments are proposed. This showcase lets you explore the design and save a sample draft in this browser.
